feat(2218): picture-first K coins page (coin stacks, cut line, candidates, DP arrow; both approaches, line debugger, light/dark)

Redesign of the LeetCode 2218 visualization so the idea reads at a glance:
coin stacks with a "top coins only" cue, a dashed cut line under the top t coins
of the current pile (+prefix[t] below it), the candidate list for the cell being
filled (t=0..T, winner starred, -inf rows crossed), and the DP table with an
arrow from the source cell dp[i-1][j-t] (purple) to the target dp[i][j] (pink).
The final step marks the chosen coins per pile (xN), the backtrack path
(approach 1) and the answer cell. One equation line under the table.

Open point 1 (approaches): kept and redesigned both. Approach 1 = 2D dp[i][j],
approach 2 = 1D dp + new_dp. They share the parser, recorder and renderer, so the
second one cost little; dropping it would remove the O(k) lesson the page had.
Open point 2 (files): moved 2218 into dedicated files, like 1970 and 3161:
public/renderer-max-coins-2218.js and public/max-coins-2218.css.

  both approaches (18 lines each, one statement per line), one step per executed
